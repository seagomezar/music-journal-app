Privacy Policy

Flute Practice Coach is designed to work without an online account. This policy explains what the app handles locally, the permissions it uses, and the choices available to you.

Effective July 24, 2026
On this page Overview Local app data Permissions Platform services & backups Sharing Retention & deletion Security Children Changes Contact

1. Overview

Flute Practice Coach (the “App”) is provided by Seagomezar (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The App is a private practice journal for flutists.

We do not operate an account service or App backend. The App contains no developer-operated advertising or in-app analytics and does not send your practice journal, scores, pitch stream, or recordings to us.

In short: we do not collect personal data through the App because the App does not transmit it to us. The separate public landing page may use optional, aggregate Plausible measurement when its deployment is configured.

2. Information handled locally

The App processes and stores information you provide in its app-managed storage, which may include:

  • Your display name and app preferences.
  • Practice routines, exercises, target tempos, and weekly goals.
  • Repertoire titles, composer names, measure progress, and notes.
  • Practice session dates, durations, history, and written notes.
  • PDF scores you choose to import.
  • Audio recordings you choose to create for self-evaluation.

This information is used only to provide the App’s features on your device. The App does not require your legal name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

In the browser version, profile and journal information is kept in browser-managed site storage. PDF importing and viewing are not available there. Browser recordings are temporary, remain available only during the active practice session, and are not saved to practice history.

3. Device permissions

Microphone

The App asks for microphone access only when you choose to use the tuner, track exercise intonation, or make a self-evaluation recording. Tuner and pitch analysis run locally on your device; the analyzed tuner audio is not saved or transmitted. You may deny or revoke this permission in system settings and continue using all features that do not require the microphone.

Files

On supported mobile platforms, when you choose to attach a score, the operating system’s file picker lets you select a PDF. The App copies the selected document into app-managed storage so it remains available with the corresponding repertoire item.

When you export or import a journal backup, the operating system’s document picker lets you choose the destination or source file. Journal backups contain routines, practice-session history, and written notes. They exclude account/profile information, audio recordings, PDF scores, and device file paths.

The App does not continuously monitor your microphone or browse files you have not selected. Tuner listening stops when you stop or hide the tuner, leave practice, or move the App to the background.

4. Platform services and backups

Apple, Google, your device manufacturer, or your mobile carrier may independently process information when you download or use apps, according to their own privacy policies. We do not control those platform services.

If you enable an operating-system backup or device-transfer feature, the platform may include some App data in that backup. Those backups are controlled by you and the platform provider—not by Flute Practice Coach. Review your device’s backup settings if you do not want app data copied to a cloud backup or another device.

5. Sale, sharing, and tracking

We do not sell personal data. We do not share App data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. The App does not use cross-app tracking technologies or serve advertisements. If enabled, the landing page sends only aggregate page and activation events through Plausible; it does not receive journal, audio, profile, or pitch data.

Because the App does not transmit your local journal to us, we cannot view or disclose it. Exported journal backups are not encrypted and may contain practice notes. Information may therefore be accessible to someone who can access your unlocked device, a device backup, or a backup file you save or share outside the App.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

App-managed information remains available until you take one of the following actions:

  • Delete an individual session, routine, or repertoire item.
  • Use Erase all data in the App’s Settings.
  • Uninstall the App.

Deleting a recorded session removes its app-managed recording. Deleting a repertoire item removes its app-managed PDF. Erasing all data removes the local profile, journal database, imported files, and recordings managed by the App.

Erase all data and uninstalling the App do not delete journal backup files you exported to Files, Downloads, cloud storage, or another provider. Delete those copies from the location where you saved them.

Deleting the App may not remove copies retained in device backups. Manage those copies through the backup service that created them. We cannot recover deleted App data or delete a backup on your behalf because we do not have access to either.

In a browser, clearing site data, using private browsing, or browser storage eviction can remove the journal. The App cannot restore browser data after it has been cleared.

7. Security

The App uses the private storage protections supplied by your operating system. No storage system is completely secure, so protect your device with a passcode, biometric lock, current software updates, and appropriate backup settings.

Do not report privacy issues by attaching private recordings, copyrighted scores, or sensitive personal information to a public GitHub issue.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Parents and guardians should supervise device access and review platform family and backup settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features, legal requirements, or platform behavior changes. The revised policy will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes will also be communicated in the App or its store listing when appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, open a request through the project’s GitHub Issues page or visit the Support page. GitHub issues are public, so do not include sensitive information.

Because we do not receive your App data, the fastest way to exercise control over it is through the App’s deletion controls, your device settings, and any backup service you use.
