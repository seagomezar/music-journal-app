Private by design

Your practice belongs to you.

Flute Practice Coach has no online account, advertising, or analytics. Your profile, journal, imported scores, and recordings are managed locally by the app.

  • The app does not upload your journal or recordings.
  • Microphone access is optional and requested only when recording.
  • Journal exports are user-controlled files and never include recordings or PDFs.
  • You can delete individual content or erase all app data from Settings.
Read the Privacy Policy

Good to know

Simple answers.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Enter a display name to create a profile stored by the app on your device.

Can I use the app without microphone access?

Yes. Recording is optional, and every non-recording feature remains available if you deny microphone permission.

What is different in the browser version?

Your profile and journal use browser storage. PDF importing and viewing are mobile-only, and browser recordings are available only during the active practice session—not in history. Clearing site data can remove the browser journal.

Does the app upload my scores or recordings?

No. The app does not operate a server or transmit your journal, PDF scores, or recordings. Your device platform may separately include app data in backups you enable.

How do I remove my information?

Delete individual sessions or repertoire items, or use Erase all data in Settings to remove the local profile and app-managed files.
