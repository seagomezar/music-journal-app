Troubleshooting

Try these steps first.

Most permission and file issues can be resolved without deleting your journal.

What is different in the browser version?

Your profile and journal remain in browser-managed site storage. PDF importing and viewing are mobile-only. Audio recorded in a browser is available during the active practice session but is not saved to history. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove the journal.

The recorder cannot start
  1. Open your device’s Settings and allow microphone access for Flute Practice Coach.
  2. Return to the App, close the recorder, and open it again.
  3. Make sure another app is not actively using the microphone.
  4. Restart the App or device if the microphone remains unavailable.

You can continue using every non-recording feature if you prefer not to grant access.

A recording stopped unexpectedly

Recording stops intentionally when you close the recorder, finish or discard a session, or send the App to the background. This protects against unintended recording. Keep the App open and the recorder visible while capturing an excerpt.

A PDF score will not open
  1. Confirm that the selected file is a valid, non-password-protected PDF.
  2. Try opening the source PDF in your device’s file viewer.
  3. Remove the repertoire item and import the score again.

Deleting the repertoire item also deletes the copy of its PDF managed by the App. It does not delete the original source file.

I changed or lost my device

Flute Practice Coach does not provide an account or cloud-sync service. Before changing devices, open Settings and choose Export journal backup. On the new device, choose Import journal backup. This transfers routines and practice history, but not recordings, PDF scores, repertoire files, or profile settings.

Your operating system may also restore some App data if you enabled its backup or device-transfer service, but restoration is not guaranteed and we cannot recover data on your behalf.

How do I erase my data?

Open the App’s Settings and choose Erase all data. This permanently removes the local profile, practice database, recordings, and PDFs managed by the App. Device backups must be managed separately through your platform provider.

Still need help?

Send a useful bug report.

Include the App version, device model, operating-system version, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Never attach private recordings, sensitive information, or copyrighted scores to a public issue.
